The Resilience Rally: Why Pending Sales are Rising in a 6.6% Market

Market Pulse: The Stability Paradox

The housing market is currently performing a surprising tightrope walk. According to the latest data, the weekly FRED average climbed to 6.51%—its highest point in nine months—yet the daily market is telling a story of defiance. Our daily survey rate held steady at 6.65% for the third consecutive day, while the 10-year Treasury yield actually softened to 4.558%.

The real headline today isn't just the rate itself, but the unexpected resilience of the American homebuyer. Despite these multi-month highs, pending home sales have risen for the third straight month. We are witnessing a clear transition from "rate shock" to "rate acceptance."

Key Drivers: The Wealth Effect and Buyer Adaptation

Why is demand rising while rates are pinned at these elevated levels? Several factors are fueling this resilience:

  1. Wall Street’s Winning Streak: As equity markets continue their "winning ways," the wealth effect is providing a significant cushion. Buyers with growing portfolios are finding themselves less sensitive to monthly payment fluctuations, keeping the prime buying season alive despite borrowing costs.
  2. Treasury Cooling: While the weekly headlines scream about nine-month highs, the 10-year Treasury yield has quietly drifted down from its 4.66% peak earlier this week. This suggests the bond market is searching for a new floor, even as CPI remains sticky at 332.407.
  3. The Refinance Divergence: Interestingly, while purchase rates stayed flat, the 30-year refinance rate jumped by 17 basis points today. This suggests that lenders are beginning to tighten margins on refinances as they prioritize the unexpectedly robust purchase market.

Strategy: Navigating the New Normal

Refinance Outlook: If you have been waiting for rates to return to 5% before acting, today’s 17-basis-point jump in refinance-specific pricing is a warning shot. Lenders are adjusting their appetite for refinances. If a move today lowers your current rate by 0.75% or more, the "guaranteed" monthly savings may outweigh the "speculative" savings of waiting for a Federal Reserve pivot that remains parked at 3.64%.

Buyer Advice: The fact that pending sales are rising at 6.65% is a signal to those on the sidelines. Competition isn't disappearing; it’s adapting. If you find a home that fits your budget and lifestyle, waiting for a major rate drop may only lead to increased competition once more buyers reach the "acceptance" phase. Use this current period of yield stabilization to lock in your rate and secure your position in a resilient market.
