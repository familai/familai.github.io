This is a clear signal that the 'sideline sitters' have seen enough. We are shifting from a 'Rate Watch' market to a 'Competition Watch' market. If application volume continues to rise by double digits, the challenge for buyers won't be the monthly payment—it will be the bidding war.

Refinance Advice: If you are currently in a high-rate loan, don't let the jump to 6.07% discourage you. We are still significantly lower than 2024 peaks. If you missed the sub-6% dip last week, keep your paperwork ready. Today's slight retreat proves that volatility works both ways, and 'mini-windows' of opportunity can open even during a Treasury yield climb.
